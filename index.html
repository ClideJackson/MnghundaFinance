About us

A lender you can talk to.

Mnghunda Finance is a Community-based lender based in Giyani, Gauteng and Mpumalanga. We started with a simple idea: borrowing money should be clear, fair and respectful.

We know that life doesn't always line up with payday. Whether you're covering school fees, a medical bill, a repair or a small business need, we're here to help you bridge the gap with a loan that makes sense for you.

When you borrow with us, you always see what you will pay back before you agree. There are no surprises and no confusing jargon. Every application is reviewed by a real person, and we take the time to understand your situation.

We keep your information private and use your documents only to assess your application. Please ask us any question before you commit.

Borrow with confidence. Repay with ease.
